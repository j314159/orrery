Orrery - Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

Overview

Orrery is a standalone Wear OS app that displays the positions of planets, the Sun, and the Moon in the sky. Your privacy is important, and this app is designed to be as minimal as possible in its data usage.

Location Data

Orrery requests access to your device's location in order to calculate the positions of celestial bodies as seen from where you are. This location data is:

  • Used entirely on-device to compute astronomical positions
  • Never transmitted to any server, third party, or external service
  • Never stored permanently — a cached copy is kept in local app storage solely to support the watch face complication, and is overwritten each time the app is opened

Data Collection

Orrery does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No network requests of any kind (all astronomical calculations are performed offline)
  • No user accounts or sign-in

Third-Party Services

Orrery uses Google Play Services solely for accessing the device's approximate location. No data is sent to Google or any other party by the app itself.

Settings

Your display preferences (such as planet style, visible bodies, and star visibility) are stored locally on your watch using standard Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Orrery does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
